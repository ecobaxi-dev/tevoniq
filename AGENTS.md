# Project direction

- TEVONIQ targets Arabic-speaking small and medium businesses. The founder currently works alone with modern tools and AI and has no client engagements to present as delivered work.
- New development and new marketing content target Arabic and English only. Do not add or translate new features/content into Turkish unless the user explicitly requests it. Preserve existing Turkish routes and content; this policy does not authorize deleting them.
- Arabic is the primary public entry language. Respect an explicitly consented visitor language preference and the enabled-language setting.
- Pesto is founded and owned by the user. Describe release availability using verified download/activation readiness. An estimated release date does not itself authorize enabling downloads or claiming availability.
- Public marketing copy uses the company voice (we help, we build), not first-person founder narration. A factual founder biography may use third person. This does not authorize claims of a staff or team.
- Do not invent staff, clients, partnerships, certifications or performance results. Label illustrative work accurately.
- See `docs/project-direction-ar.md` for the current owner decisions and `docs/sme-site-inventory-ar.md` for the proposed content inventory. The inventory is a recommendation, not blanket authorization to delete its contents.
